# Privacy Policy - THE QS HUB

Last updated: May 2026

The platform may collect account data, subscription data, uploaded drawings, project outputs, usage logs and technical logs.

Data is used to provide quantity takeoff services, store projects, manage billing, enforce limits, diagnose issues, improve reliability and protect the platform.

Data is not sold. Limited data may be processed by necessary providers such as Dodo Payments, hosting/database providers, storage providers, monitoring providers and AI providers.

Production deployments should isolate user files by account and use private cloud storage with restricted access.

Users may request correction, export or deletion of available account/project data subject to operational, legal, security and billing obligations.
