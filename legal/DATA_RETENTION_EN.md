# Data Retention Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Projects, files and generated reports are retained to provide the service and project history.

Usage logs are retained to enforce plan limits, support operations, diagnose issues and protect the platform.

Temporary files and caches should be cleaned regularly.

Users may request deletion of their data unless legal, billing, security or operational obligations require retention.

Deleted data may remain in backups until the backup retention cycle expires.
