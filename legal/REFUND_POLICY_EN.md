# Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Subscriptions are billed in advance. Users may cancel future renewals through the payment portal or support.

Refunds may be reviewed when a duplicate charge occurred, subscription activation failed due to a verified technical issue, or a major service failure occurred shortly after payment and could not be resolved.

Refunds are generally not guaranteed after meaningful use of projects, AI calls or exports, or where results were affected by poor/incomplete drawings or unreviewed engineering use.

Payments, refunds and cancellations are processed through Dodo Payments or the approved payment provider.
